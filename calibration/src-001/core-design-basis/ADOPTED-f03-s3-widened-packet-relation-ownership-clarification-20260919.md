I clarify the adopted F-03 accepted-worker-return production-reachability design for cumulative run format `1.9.0-provisional` and capability `orchestrator-work-transitions` as follows.

The C-05 dedicated post-S2 packet-widening family creates a new exact PKT while the run remains in S3. That packet receives a fresh S3-owned semantic subject because its exact bytes differ from its predecessor packet basis.

If that exact S3-owned widened-packet semantic proposal contains relation proposals required by the existing semantic output contract, those relation proposals MUST remain owned by the same S3 semantic subject.

They MUST NOT be reassigned to S2 merely to satisfy the predecessor stage/source-kind restriction.

For new cumulative `1.9.0-provisional` runs using `orchestrator-work-transitions`, Core therefore permits a narrowly bounded relation-proposal exception:

`owner_stage = S3`
with
`source_kind = PKT`

is legal only when the relation proposal belongs to the fresh semantic subject of a PKT created by the dedicated C-05 post-S2 packet-widening work family.

This exception does not generally permit PKT-sourced S3 relation proposals.

## Exact eligibility

An S3 PKT-sourced relation proposal is legal only when Core can mechanically prove all of the following:

- the run uses `1.9.0-provisional`;
- `orchestrator-work-transitions` is active;
- the current stage is S3;
- the source PKT was created by the exact authenticated C-05 post-S2 widening work family;
- the widening work item is bound to a retained valid S3 normalizer widening request;
- the PKT is the fresh exact successor/additional packet produced by that work;
- the packet semantic subject is fresh and owned by S3;
- the relation proposal occurs inside that exact semantic subject;
- the relation proposal's `owner_stage` equals the semantic subject's `owner_stage`;
- the relation proposal's `source_kind = PKT`;
- the relation proposal's `source_id` equals one of the exact PKT identities belonging to that widened-packet subject;
- its basis packet IDs and review-subject digest bind the same exact subject and packet evidence;
- all ordinary relation-family, relation-type, target, material-use and structural predicates continue to pass.

A caller, worker, adapter, or parent model may not opt an arbitrary S3 packet relation into this exception.

## Relation taxonomy remains strict

This clarification does not make every relation type legal for a PKT source.

Existing relation source-kind constraints remain controlling.

In particular, any relation family/type that already requires a CC source continues to require a CC source.

A widened PKT MUST NOT be used as the source of a claim-only relation merely because its semantic subject is S3-owned.

At minimum:

- `semantic-prerequisite` remains CC-sourced;
- `claim-dependency` forms that require CC remain CC-sourced;
- any other existing relation predicate requiring `source_kind = CC` remains unchanged.

The bounded exception applies only where the existing relation taxonomy already permits a PKT source and the only conflict is the blanket predecessor rule that S3 proposals must have a CC source.

Do not weaken the general `sourceKindLegal` relation predicate.

Instead, narrow the stage/source-kind predicate so that the otherwise-legal PKT relation is admitted only under the authenticated C-05 widened-packet context.

## No S2 reassignment

A relation proposal produced as part of the fresh S3 widened-packet semantic subject MUST NOT be labeled `owner_stage = S2`.

The original S2 seal remains immutable.

The old predecessor packet's S2 semantic subject and relation proposals remain historical.

Fresh widened packet bytes require fresh S3 semantic review and fresh S3 relation proposal ownership.

No new S2 semantic subject, S2 relation proposal, S2 receipt, or S2 seal is created.

## Proposal versus canonical relation

These S3 packet relations remain retained relation proposals only.

This clarification does NOT authorize direct canonical REL creation in S3.

Canonical relation serialization remains subject to the existing downstream relation-review and S4 closure contracts.

The proposal must retain:

- exact relation family/type;
- exact source PKT;
- exact target;
- exact record state;
- exact null reason;
- exact basis packet IDs;
- exact producer identity;
- exact review-subject digest;

## Fresh review

Any relation proposal inside it remains part of that reviewed semantic subject under the existing semantic-review coverage requirements.

Where the relation contract separately requires fresh relation review before canonicalization, that requirement remains unchanged.

No reviewer may manufacture or rewrite a relation to make the proposal legal.
