# K2.20 — duplicate comparison and admission accounting

This is the deterministic, read-only Slice 8 contract for cumulative capability
`duplicate-overlap-review`, introduced by `1.8.0-provisional`. Retained 1.0–1.7
runs retain their original interpretation and pins. Literal marker strings in
frozen corpus bytes are data; operative duplicate records on predecessor
control or ledger surfaces are incompatible.

Core constructs the S3-closed catalogue in durable lineage-current CC order.
Discovery records bind its exact claim/lineage prefix, catalogue, actual shown
windows, accepted producer bindings, explicit candidates, independent L5 sweep
windows and unresolved findings. Entry coverage is distinct from examining
cross-window combinations, and neither establishes semantic recall. Empty
candidate inventories still require discovery and sweep accounting.

Comparison subjects bind every member's immutable CC bytes and admitting SEM,
exact evidence, source identity, declared ancestry, incident proposed relations,
working ambiguity and material context. Closure follows declared references;
it does not infer semantic relevance or traverse unrelated relation
neighbourhoods. Oversized or missing required context cannot be silently
truncated. The seventeen distinction dimensions are structural coverage
labels. Complete occurrences, member mappings and SEM unit occurrence mappings
must match the mechanically reconstructed union.

The immutable subject binds the proposal, basis, field enumeration, reservation,
run pins, producer, exact prompt parts, reviewer profile and context manifest.
The reviewer sees exactly the sealed subject and its declared material assets.
Only the fixed task `Challenge only the attached sealed duplicate comparison
under L3.` selects the duplicate L3 charter. L3R relation authority is preserved.

Comparison outcomes are `duplicate`, `overlap`, `distinct` and
`CANNOT_DETERMINE`; reviewer verdicts are `upheld`, `refuted` and
`cannot-determine`. Contradictions use `contradiction_pairs`. No additional
comparison outcome or relation family is introduced.

One fresh round-1 L3 is required. Upheld or refuted forbids round 2.
Cannot-determine requires exactly one fresh round-2 review of the identical
subject. Round-2 refuted yields aggregate refuted; either other verdict leaves
aggregate cannot-determine. There is no third round, majority vote or additional
reviewer. Absorption after indeterminacy requires new legal frozen basis/context,
a new proposal and a new subject. A new ID or omitted predecessor link does not
erase the earlier uncertainty.

Absorption requires all adopted predicates together: an open S4 window;
unchanged exact subject, delivery and reservation; round-1 and DDR upheld;
duplicate/new-successor; no blocking finding, contradiction, unknown origin or
separate-claims distinction; binary reviewed origin; complete unchanged
provenance; fresh S4 normalizer/SEM/L2S; required independent L2F; and matching
canonical after-images. One composed duplicate/semantic/material transaction
writes the new CC, LIN, merge-map, USE, semantic resolution and DUE. Neither a
predecessor identity nor final L3 prose becomes the successor.

The six duplicate tables preserve discoveries, proposals, assignments, results,
decisions and effects. Each candidate remains accounted for, each DUP has at
most one final DUE, and an assigned withdrawal cannot bypass required results.
Refuted, unknown and failed-successor histories remain inspectable. An honest
unresolved run can be structurally valid while absorption remains forbidden.

S4 order is:

1. S3 closure, discovery and independent L5 sweep.
2. Comparison proposals and fresh L3.
3. Eligible successor expression, L2S and applicable L2F.
4. One composed canonical transaction.
5. Relation reconciliation and L3R.
6. C1 relation closure with material, semantic and
   `duplicate_review_closure_hash` seals.
7. C2, C3, then S5.

The duplicate seal hashes the complete duplicate ledger and appears exactly once
inside the retained C1 event. Before S4, absence is valid and duplicate writes
are forbidden. On S4 entry the ledger is required, including zero-candidate
runs. At C1 all actual candidates/proposals/effects must be accounted for.
After C1 duplicate-related writes are refused before changing bytes.

Précis section 17 mechanically retains rows
`{proposal_id,subject_digest,member_ids,finding_ref,review_verdict,effect}` for
producer/reviewer unknowns, refutations, flagged contradiction assertions and
failed successors, including history behind later success.

Stable refusal tokens are `DUP_FORMAT`, `DUP_ENUM`, `DUP_REFERENCE`,
`DUP_EVIDENCE`, `DUP_SUBJECT`, `DUP_REVIEW`, `DUP_ISOLATION`, `DUP_STATE`,
`DUP_ACCOUNTING`, `DUP_WINDOW` and `DUP_COMPATIBILITY`.

K2.20 calls no model, network, OCR or renderer. It does not decide semantic
equivalence, paraphrase meaning, materiality, qualifier entailment, condition
subsumption, contradiction from text/numbers, support independence,
representative wording, ideal claim count, recall, context adequacy, reviewer
quality or cognitive freshness. Same-text distinct/unknown, different-text
duplicate and deliberately semantically wrong complete declarations must remain
structurally admissible. Fixture declarations are not reference answers.

Manual mode remains the only sanctioned execution mode. Structural checks and
fixture/process/runtime tests grant no semantic validation, sanction,
acceptance, production readiness, golden status or v1, and do not close F-03.
